# 图表解读

Read the chart title, axis labels, units, legend and footnotes before interpreting marks. Check whether axes are logarithmic, truncated, reversed or shared; in dual-axis plots associate each series with its own axis. Distinguish individual values, cumulative totals and percentages.

Crop marks together with their tick context and series identity. Prefer printed data labels over pixel estimates. When only a plotted position is available, report approximate values with precision justified by the axis spacing. Preserve uncertainty intervals and distinguish them from the central estimate.

For derived comparisons, identify the numerator, denominator and time baseline explicitly. Do not substitute percentage-point differences for percent change. Separate visual association from causal claims. Answer with the relevant series, values, units and concise supporting calculation, rather than describing every mark.

Finish with the runtime action=answer object. Its answer field is a string containing the requested output.
